# Changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-18
- A monster you hit keeps fighting you when another player's game takes it over (frequent with Network Performance System): who hit it is now stored on the monster itself.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-18
- Monsters ignore a player whose armor is at least one biome ahead of theirs. The weakest of helmet, chest and legs sets the player's tier.
- Greydwarfs count as Meadows monsters: Bronze, Troll or Bear armor is enough. Their Brute and Shaman stay Black Forest.
- A monster hit by a player fights back, and forgets them after 30 s without a blow either way.
- Boars, starred monsters, raid monsters, bosses and sea serpents always attack. Deer still flee.
- Settings `Prestige.*`, `Armor.Tier1-8` and `Monsters.Tier1-8`, synced from the server.
