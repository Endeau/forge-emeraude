# Emeraude Construction

While building, Q and E cycle through the snap points of the piece you are placing, and the game keeps that choice
until you go all the way around again. This mod adds one key to put snapping straight back to automatic.

- **Backspace** (default): snapping back to automatic. The usual "Snapping: automatic" message confirms it.
- Change the key in `BepInEx/config/emeraude.construction.cfg`, setting `Snapping.ResetSnapKey`.

Client-side only: other players and the server do not need it.

---

**FR** — En construction, Q et E font défiler les points d'accroche de la pièce. La touche **Retour arrière** remet
l'accroche en automatique d'un coup. Touche modifiable : `Snapping.ResetSnapKey`. Mod client, inutile sur le serveur.
