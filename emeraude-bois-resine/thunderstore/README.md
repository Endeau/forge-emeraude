# Emeraude Bois Resine

Plain wood rots: a wood piece left in the rain without a roof, or standing in water, loses 5 % of its health every minute, down to half. This mod adds a resined version of each plain wood building piece that doesn't.

## The pieces

Hammer, *Resined wood* tab. Every building piece that costs only wood and wears out in the rain gets a copy: floors, walls, beams, poles, stairs, ladder, doors, gate, and so on. The list is built at startup from the game's own pieces.

- **Cost:** twice the wood of the original, at the same crafting station. Deconstructing gives everything back.
- **No water wear:** rain and water no longer damage them. Health, support and strength stay the same as the original.
- **Look:** slightly darker, amber wood. The name ends with *(resined)*.

Existing buildings don't change: replace the pieces you want to protect with their resined version.

Side effect from the game: a resined piece never counts as wet, so it can catch fire in the rain like dry wood. It still can't burn under water.

## Installation

Requires [Jötunn](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/ValheimModding/Jotunn/). Install it on the **server and every player's game**: the resined pieces are new pieces, players without the mod can't join.
