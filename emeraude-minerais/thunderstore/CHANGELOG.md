# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-23
- New counter `mined`: ore and scrap picked up from the ground by each player. Items dropped by a player (or spilled from a destroyed chest or ship) don't count when picked up again.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23
- Counts, per player, the ore put in a smelter or blast furnace, the bars that come out of it, and the bars spent crafting, upgrading or building.
- The server saves the totals in `BepInEx/config/emeraude-minerais/classement.tsv`.
